What car is this?

Start with the visual clues in the photo, make a thoughtful first pass, then use an AI-assisted identification as a faster way to narrow the answer.

Identify it with RevvRadar Use the clues

Answer the question in the order that makes a car distinct.

When someone asks “What car is this?”, they are usually reacting to one memorable detail: a rare-looking shape, a badge they cannot read, or a sound paired with a vehicle that passed too quickly. The best approach is to work from the strongest clues to the smaller differences.

01 · MAKE

Look for the clearest manufacturer cue.

A visible badge, grille shape, headlight signature, or wheel-center logo may be enough to identify the make before you consider the model.

02 · BODY AND PROPORTION

Read the silhouette.

Count doors, notice the roofline, compare hood length to cabin length, and look at the rear window and wheel arches. These clues often separate models from the same maker.

03 · GENERATION AND TRIM

Check the small revisions.

Bumpers, lights, exhaust outlets, vents, and badging can help distinguish a generation or performance variant when the broad answer is already clear.

Why a single photo can be misleading.

Perspective can make a compact coupe look longer, poor lighting can obscure a badge, and customized parts can make a familiar model look uncommon. If the result does not fit what you see, use another angle rather than forcing a confident answer from an incomplete photo.

A front-three-quarter photo is often useful because it shows the grille, lights, hood, doors, and wheels together. The rear is especially valuable when the model badge is readable. For an interior view, look for the dash shape, instrument display, and steering wheel badge.
