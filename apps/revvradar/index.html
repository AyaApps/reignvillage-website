Frequently asked questions

Before you download.

Clear answers about access, privacy, AI accuracy, and availability.

How does RevvRadar protect location privacy?

RevvRadar uses controls such as approximate location display, private captures, profile privacy, and reduced or hidden location details around sensitive places. Location access can be disabled in iOS Settings. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Can AI results be wrong?

Yes. Results depend on image quality, lighting, vehicle angle, and the AI model. RevvRadar makes results reviewable and provides a way to suggest a correction when something looks wrong.

Are captures public?

Not automatically in every case. Members can use private profile and capture controls, and captures made without location remain private unless the member chooses to share them.

Does RevvRadar show events near me?

Yes. RevvRadar includes automotive event discovery with map placement and details such as location, time, cost, and event type where available.

Is RevvRadar free?

Yes. The Free tier includes three vehicle captures per day, additional captures through rewarded ads, and access to the map, events, and community features. Optional Pro and Premium plans raise the daily capture limit.

Is Android available?

RevvRadar is currently available for iPhone. Android is not available at this time and may be considered later based on demand.
