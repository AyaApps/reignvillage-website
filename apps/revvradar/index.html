Frequently asked questions

Before you download.

A few details about captures, privacy, and Free access.

Is RevvRadar free?

Yes. Get six daily captures and all available vehicle specifications on Free. Optional rewarded ads let you refill your allowance when available. Pro and Premium offer higher limits, more room for saved photos, and additional appearance options.

Can identification results be wrong?

Yes. Identifications, years, specifications, and values are estimates and may be wrong. Review the result and suggest a correction if something looks off.

How does RevvRadar protect location privacy?

RevvRadar uses controls such as approximate location display, private captures, profile privacy, and reduced or hidden location details around sensitive places. Location access can be disabled in iOS Settings. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
